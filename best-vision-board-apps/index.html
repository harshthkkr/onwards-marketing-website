The 7 best vision board apps for 2027

Onwards

Best for boards you act on, and for couples, friends and families. Onwards is our app, so read this entry with that in mind. It is built around what happens after the board is made. You save places, posts and ideas from Instagram, the web or any app, each dream breaks into Little Steps with Plan AI, and Shared Spaces let a partner, friends or family add to the same board and tick things off together.

  • Good at: turning each dream into small steps, and sharing one board between two or more people.
  • Keep in mind: it is not a design tool. If you want a decorated poster to print, a design app suits you better.
  • Cost and devices: free with no ads, on iPhone and Android.

Canva

Best for a designed board to print or use as a wallpaper. Canva is a general design tool with a free vision board maker. You start from a template, drag in photos and text, and download the result as an image or PDF. It has a large library of free stock photos and graphics, and you can invite others to design with you.

  • Good at: a polished single image for your wall, lock screen or desktop.
  • Keep in mind: the result is a picture. It will not remind you of next steps or track what you have done.
  • Cost and devices: free to use, with some tools reserved for the paid Pro plan. Works on the web and in its mobile apps.

Pinterest

Best for collecting inspiration. Many vision boards start on Pinterest. You save Pins to a board, and a group board lets you invite other people to add to it. It is the easiest place to find images for almost any dream.

  • Good at: finding and saving pictures quickly, alone or with collaborators.
  • Keep in mind: a Pinterest board is a collection, not a plan, and the feed of new ideas makes it easy to keep browsing.
  • Cost and devices: free, on the web, iPhone and Android.

Vision Board – Perfectly Happy

Best for affirmations and a daily visualisation routine. Perfectly Happy is a dedicated vision board app with a manifestation focus. It offers two built-in image libraries plus in-app web search, a library of 6,000 affirmations, and it can combine your images, affirmations and music into a vision board video. It also includes a gratitude journal and a mood tracker.

  • Good at: a short daily practice of looking at your board with affirmations and music.
  • Keep in mind: it is built for one person’s daily routine more than for planning with someone else.
  • Cost and devices: a seven-day free trial, then a paid subscription. On iPhone, iPad and Android.

Vizzy

Best for seeing your board on your iPhone home screen. Vizzy is a vision board maker for iPhone and iPad. Its main draw is the widget: you can add your board to your home screen so you see it every time you unlock your phone. It also offers daily affirmations and reminders tied to your board.

  • Good at: keeping your board in sight through a home screen widget.
  • Keep in mind: its App Store listing covers iPhone and iPad only.
  • Cost and devices: free to download with in-app purchases, on iPhone and iPad.

Milanote

Best for a freeform board on a big screen. Milanote is a visual workspace used by designers, with a free vision board template. You drag images, notes, video and colour swatches onto an open canvas, search a built-in library of free photos, and share the board with a link.

  • Good at: a flexible, good-looking board with notes beside your pictures.
  • Keep in mind: it is a general creative tool, so there is nothing specific to goals or progress.
  • Cost and devices: free to start, with a paid plan for heavier use. Web, desktop, iPad and mobile apps.

Notion

Best if you already plan your life in Notion. Notion’s template marketplace lists hundreds of vision board templates made by its community, many of them free. They work well if your goals, habits and notes already live in Notion and you want your board beside them.

  • Good at: keeping a vision board next to your goals, trackers and notes.
  • Keep in mind: it takes some setting up, and Notion is built for documents more than pictures.
  • Cost and devices: Notion has a free plan and many free templates. Web, desktop and mobile apps.

How to choose a vision board app

Start with what you want the board to do for you.

  • You want something beautiful to print or set as a wallpaper. Use a design tool such as Canva.
  • You want to gather ideas before you decide. Start on Pinterest, then move your favourites to a board you will keep.
  • You want a daily visualisation habit. Choose a dedicated app with affirmations, such as Perfectly Happy, or a home screen widget, such as Vizzy.
  • You want to act on the board through the year. Choose an app that attaches steps to each dream, such as Onwards.
  • You are making it with a partner, friends or family. Choose something you can all add to: Onwards, a Pinterest group board or a shared Canva design.

Vision board app or design tool?

A design tool gives you a finished picture. That is all many people want, and a printed board on the wall is hard to ignore.

A vision board app keeps the board alive: you can add to it, change it as the year changes, and see what you have done. If boards you made before were forgotten by February, the second kind is worth trying. Our guide on how to make a vision board covers both.

The best vision board app for couples and families

Most vision board apps are made for one person. For two or more, look for a shared board that everyone can add to from their own phone, and a way to see who is doing what.

Onwards was built for this, with Shared Spaces for couples, friends and families. A Pinterest group board works for collecting pictures together. Our couples vision board guide has a method that works in any of them.

Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

What is the best free vision board app?

It depends on what you want. Onwards is free with no ads and is built for boards you act on and share. Pinterest is free for collecting inspiration, and Canva’s free plan is enough to design a board to print.

What is the best vision board app for couples?

Look for a shared board both of you can add to from your own phones. Onwards has Shared Spaces made for couples, friends and families, with steps you can tick off together. A Pinterest group board is a simple way to collect pictures as a pair.

Is Canva good for vision boards?

Yes, if you want a finished image. Canva has free vision board templates, stock photos and an easy editor, and you can download the result to print or use as a wallpaper. It does not track steps or progress after the board is made.

Which vision board app has an iPhone widget?

Vizzy lets you add your vision board to your iPhone home screen as a widget, according to its App Store listing. Check the listing for the current features and price.

Do I need an app to make a vision board?

No. A poster board, printed photos and glue work well. An app helps if you want your board with you, want to change it through the year, or want to share it. See how to make a vision board for both ways.
