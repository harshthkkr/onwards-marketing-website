Good questions. Clear answers.

The things you’re probably wondering.

What changes with Onwards?

It gives “we should” somewhere to become “we did.” Make the dream visible, take the next step, and let your people keep it alive.

Who is Onwards for?

Use it personally or with your circle—family, friends, partners, siblings, travel crews, or anyone you trust to root for you.

What can we do inside?

Dreams shape the life ahead. Saves gather inspiration from anywhere. Little Joys turn everyday intentions into shared action.

Where does AI fit?

Speak it with Voice AI. See it with Image AI. Shape it with Plan AI. You stay in control; AI simply makes starting lighter.

Is it private and pressure-free?

Yes. No public scores, comparison, targeted ads, or selling personal information. Your spaces belong to the people you choose.

How do I get Onwards?

V1 is heading to iOS and Android first, with web and MCP on the road ahead. Founding Members get the first look—just say hello on WhatsApp. Join the Founding 100 →
