Quick answer: Good vision board ideas for 2027 cover every area of life: travel, home, career and money, health, relationships, personal growth and fun. Pick four to six areas, choose specific pictures and words for each, and add one next step per dream.

How to make a vision board for 2027

The short version is below. For the full walkthrough, read how to make a vision board in 7 steps, or see 12 vision board examples for layouts you can copy.

  1. Reflect first. What worked in 2026, what didn’t, and what do you want more of?
  2. Pick your areas. Choose four to six from the list below so the board covers your whole life, not just work.
  3. Collect pictures and words. Save images from Instagram, Pinterest and the web, or use your own photos. Choose the ones that make you feel something.
  4. Arrange it. Put the dream that matters most in the middle and build around it.
  5. Give each picture a next step. This is what turns a vision board into a plan. “Run a 10K” becomes “sign up for a race in April.”
  6. Keep it where you’ll see it. On a wall, as your lock screen, or in an app you open every day.

Travel and adventure vision board ideas

Start with the places. A photo of somewhere you want to stand is one of the strongest images you can put on a board.

  • The one trip you keep postponing
  • A country you’ve never been to
  • A city break for every season
  • A solo trip, just for you
  • A long hike, trail or pilgrimage
  • A road trip with no fixed plan
  • Northern Lights, cherry blossoms or a desert sky
  • Learning a few phrases before you go

Home and lifestyle ideas

How do you want your everyday life to look and feel?

  • The home you want to live in, room by room
  • A reading corner or a desk with a view
  • A tidy, calm morning routine
  • A garden, balcony or a single thriving plant
  • Sunday dinners at your own table
  • The neighbourhood you want to wake up in
  • A pet you’ve wanted for years

Career and money ideas

Be specific here: a number, a title or a date gives your board something to aim at.

  • The job title or project you’re working towards
  • A savings number and the date you’ll reach it
  • Paying off one debt for good
  • Starting the side project or business
  • A course, certificate or skill to finish
  • A raise or a rate you’ll ask for
  • A career break or sabbatical
  • An emergency fund that lets you sleep

Health and wellbeing ideas

Pick images of how you want to feel, not just how you want to look.

  • A race, a distance or a first pull-up
  • Learning to hold a handstand
  • Eight hours of sleep, most nights
  • Cooking at home four nights a week
  • A daily walk, whatever the weather
  • Less screen time in the evening
  • A yoga, swim or dance class you look forward to
  • A calmer mind: journalling, therapy or meditation

Love, family and friendship ideas

The people on your board matter as much as the places.

  • A weekly date night you protect
  • More time with parents or grandparents
  • A friends trip that finally happens
  • Being the friend who remembers birthdays
  • A shared dream with your partner
  • Hosting people more often
  • A family tradition worth starting

Personal growth ideas

Things that are only for you.

  • Twelve books in twelve months
  • Learning a language or an instrument
  • Saying no without apologising
  • A creative project finished, not just started
  • Volunteering for something you care about
  • A fear you face this year
  • A skill you’ve put off since school

Fun and creativity ideas

A board with no play on it is just a to-do list.

  • A festival, concert or match to be at
  • A pottery, painting or photography class
  • Trying one new restaurant a month
  • A hobby with no goal attached
  • A birthday you plan properly this time
  • More spontaneous weekends

Words, quotes and themes for 2027

A single word can tie the whole board together.

  • A word for the year: “braver,” “slower,” “closer”
  • One sentence about who you’re becoming
  • A quote you actually believe
  • Three feelings you want more of
  • One thing you’re leaving behind in 2026

What pictures to put on a vision board

The best vision board pictures are specific and personal. A stock photo of “success” does very little; a photo of the exact street you want to live on does a lot.

  • Your own photos of moments you want more of
  • Places: the view, the street, the front door
  • People doing the thing you want to do
  • Numbers and dates written large
  • Words and short phrases in your own handwriting
  • Screenshots of saved posts that keep catching your eye

Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

What should I put on my vision board?

Pictures and words for the areas of life that matter to you: travel, home, career and money, health, relationships, personal growth and fun. Aim for a handful of specific dreams in each area, not a wall of everything.

When should I make a vision board for 2027?

Any time works, but late December and early January are popular because you’re already reflecting on the year. The important part is revisiting it: a monthly check-in keeps it alive past January.

How do I make a digital vision board?

Collect images on your phone as you find them and arrange them in an app. Onwards is a free vision board app that lets you save from any app and turns each dream into small next steps.

Do vision boards actually work?

A board on its own won’t do the work. What helps is being clear about what you want, seeing it often, and attaching a small next step to each dream. That’s the difference between wishing and planning.
