01

Essence

Rooting for each other's dreams — and doing something about them.

Warm, not soft Encouraging and human, but always moving. Onwards celebrates progress, not perfection.
Playful, not childish Clean white space, but with warmth — sunny gradients, soft shadows, cheerful color. Delight without the noise.
Together, always Every surface assumes two or more people. Language says "we", cheers beat streaks.

Iconography

Outline icons, 1.75px stroke, rounded caps and joins, on a 24px grid (use Lucide or Phosphor Regular). Ink at rest — Ember only when active or celebrating. Never filled, never two-tone.

06

Voice & tone

A supportive partner, not a productivity coach. First-person plural, plain words, small celebrations. Never guilt, never streak-pressure, never corporate.

SAY "Japan trip just moved to Planning — nice." "Beena cheered you on 🎉" "One small step this week?"
DON'T SAY "You haven't logged progress in 5 days!" "Unlock premium to achieve more." "Optimize your goal pipeline."
07

UI expression

White cards on white canvas, soft red-tinted shadows for lift, 20–28px radius, pill buttons. Hero moments wear the sunny gradient; everyday cards stay clean. Ember is the single primary action per view.
