Quick answer: A couples vision board is a shared collection of images and words for the life two partners want to build. To make one: dream separately, share and find the overlap, pick your life areas, collect images, and give every picture a next step.

What is a couples vision board?

A couples vision board is a shared picture of the life you want to build together: the home, the trips, the habits and the feelings, collected as images and words in one place. Unlike a solo vision board, it’s a conversation. Making one together shows you where your dreams overlap, and gives you both something to keep coming back to.

How to make a couples vision board in 6 steps

  1. Set the scene. Pick a relaxed evening with no phones (except for saving pictures). Snacks help.
  2. Dream separately for 15 minutes. Each of you writes or collects what you want in the next one, three and five years.
  3. Share and find the overlap. Read your lists to each other. The overlap goes in the middle of the board, and individual dreams go around it, because supporting each other’s goals matters too.
  4. Choose your categories. Use the prompts below to make sure you haven’t forgotten money, health or friends.
  5. Collect images that feel right. Save photos from Instagram, Pinterest and the web, or use your own. Pick images that make you both feel something.
  6. Turn each picture into a next step. This is the step most boards skip. “House by the sea” becomes “visit three coastal towns this year.” A board with next steps gets lived; a board without them gets framed.

Couples vision board categories and prompts

Use these eight areas as a checklist. You don’t need all of them, but talking through each one surfaces dreams you might not have said out loud.

  • HomeWhere do you want to live in five years? What does a perfect Sunday at home look like?
  • TravelWhich three places would you regret never seeing together?
  • MoneyWhat would make you both feel calm about money? A savings goal, a debt paid off, a date to stop renting?
  • HealthHow do you want to feel in your bodies? Shared habits like walks, cooking or a race to train for.
  • RelationshipHow do you want to treat each other? Date nights, rituals, how you handle hard weeks.
  • Career and growthWhat is each of you working towards, and how will the other one support it?
  • Family and friendsKids, pets, parents, and the friends you want to see more of.
  • FunThe things that are just for joy: hobbies, adventures, festivals, a dog you’ll name together.

Couples vision board ideas to get you started

  • A photo of the kind of home you want, down to the kitchen
  • A map with pins for your next three trips
  • A savings number and the date you’ll hit it
  • A weekly date-night ritual you both protect
  • A race, hike or challenge you’ll train for together
  • The dog, garden or studio you keep talking about
  • A word for the year, like “slower,” “braver” or “closer”
  • One dream each that the other promises to cheer on

Paper or digital: which is better for couples?

Paper boards are lovely to make together and look great on a wall, but they’re hard to update and easy to stop noticing.

A digital vision board lives on both your phones, so you can add ideas the moment you see them, wherever you each are. That makes it a good fit for long-distance couples too. The best digital boards also help you act on what’s on them.

Many couples do both: a paper board for the ritual of making it, and an app for the day-to-day.

How to keep your vision board alive

  • Check in once a month: what moved, what’s next?
  • Celebrate every dream you reach, however small.
  • Swap out dreams that no longer fit. Changing your mind is part of it.
  • Rebuild it every year, ideally in January when everyone else is dreaming too.

Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

What do you put on a couples vision board?

Images and words for the life you want together: home, travel, money, health, your relationship, careers, family and fun. Include shared dreams in the middle and one or two personal dreams each that the other person supports.

Should couples make a vision board together or separately?

Both. Dream separately first so each voice is heard, then build the board together around where your dreams overlap.

Is there an app for a couples vision board?

Yes. Onwards is a free couples app with a shared, digital vision board. You can save images from any app, and Plan AI turns each dream into small steps you can both work on.

How often should we update our vision board?

Glance at it weekly, check in properly once a month, and rebuild it once a year. A board you update is a board you use.
