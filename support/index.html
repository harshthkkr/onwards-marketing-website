Onwards Support

For account, sign-in, privacy, and deletion assistance.

Contact us

Email devanshujain95@gmail.com from the address connected to your Onwards account when possible. Do not email your password, verification code, recovery code, private documents, or authentication tokens.

Sign-in or verification

Tell us whether the problem involves email/password or Google Sign-In and include the exact non-sensitive error message shown by the app.

Password recovery

Use “Forgot password?” in the app first. If no code arrives, check spam and confirm you entered the account email correctly.

Privacy request

Use the subject “Onwards privacy request” and describe whether you want access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion.

Account deletion

Email from the account address with the subject “Onwards account deletion request.” We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

Helpful details to include

  • Your account email address.
  • Whether you use iOS or Android.
  • The approximate time the issue occurred.
  • A description of what you expected and what happened.

Please redact private content and security credentials from screenshots. Onwards will never ask you to send a password or one-time code by email.
