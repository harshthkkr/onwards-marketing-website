Privacy & your account

Privacy request

Use the subject “Onwards privacy request” and tell us whether you want access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion.

Delete your account

Email from your account address with the subject “Onwards account deletion request.” We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

Reporting a problem? These details help

  • Your account email address.
  • Whether you use iOS or Android.
  • Roughly when it happened.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.

Please leave out passwords, verification codes, and private content (blur screenshots if needed). Onwards will never ask you to send a password or one-time code by email.
