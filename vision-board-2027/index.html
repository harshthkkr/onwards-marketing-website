Collect pictures and words for them, arrange them on a board or in an app, and add a first step and a month to every dream. It takes an hour or two, and late December to early January is the most popular time.

What is a 2027 vision board?

A 2027 vision board is a collection of pictures and words that shows what you want your life to look like by the end of 2027. It can be a poster on a wall, a pin board, a phone wallpaper or a board in an app.

The year in the name matters. A board with a deadline is easier to act on than a board of someday dreams, because every picture on it has twelve months to become real.

When to make your 2027 vision board

Most people make theirs between Christmas and the second week of January, when they are already thinking about the year ahead. Earlier is fine: a board made in November or December gives you time to line up first steps before January starts.

There is no deadline. A board made in February still covers most of the year. Set aside an hour or two, and a little longer if you are making it with someone.

Look back at 2026 first

Ten minutes on the year that is ending makes a better board. Answer these on paper before you choose a single picture.

  • What am I proud of from 2026? Big or small. It shows what you are capable of.
  • What gave me energy, and what drained it? Put more of the first on your board and plan around the second.
  • What did I say “next year” about? Next year is here. Those belong on the board.
  • What did I start and not finish? Decide whether to carry it into 2027 or let it go.
  • What do I want an ordinary week to feel like? Not every picture has to be a milestone.

2027 vision board ideas for every area of life

Choose four to six areas and one or two dreams in each. These are starting points, not a checklist.

Travel and adventure

  • One big trip, with the month you will go
  • A weekend away every season
  • A place you have talked about for years
  • A first: first solo trip, first hike, first road trip

Home

  • The room you will finally finish
  • A move, with the neighbourhood named
  • A deposit amount and a date
  • A home that is easier to keep tidy

Career and money

  • The role or project you want by December
  • A savings number for the year
  • A debt paid off
  • A skill or qualification finished

Health and wellbeing

  • An event to train for, with its date
  • A sleep routine you keep on weekdays
  • Meals you cook at home each week
  • Time outside every day

Relationships

  • A weekly ritual with your partner
  • Friends you will see every month
  • A family trip or tradition
  • A hard conversation you will stop putting off

Growth and fun

  • Books you will read in 2027
  • A language, instrument or craft
  • A hobby that has nothing to do with work
  • Something you will do just because you enjoy it

How to make a 2027 vision board in 6 steps

  1. Reflect on 2026. Use the questions above. What you want more of and less of tells you what belongs on the board.
  2. Pick four to six areas. Travel, home, career and money, health, relationships, growth and fun. A few areas keep the board about your whole life.
  3. Write specific dreams. “Travel more” becomes “Lisbon in May.” “Save money” becomes a number and a month.
  4. Collect pictures and words. Start with your own photos, then add saved posts and images from the web or magazines.
  5. Arrange it. Put the dream that matters most in the middle. Leave space so the board is easy to read at a glance.
  6. Add a first step to each dream, and a month. “Renew passport in January.” This is what turns a 2027 vision board into a 2027 plan.

Words for a 2027 vision board

Many people choose one word for the year and put it at the centre of the board. Pick the one that describes how you want 2027 to feel.

  • Begin
  • Steady
  • Brave
  • Together
  • Enough
  • Build
  • Lighter
  • Present
  • Finish
  • Explore
  • Rest
  • Onwards

Three layouts that work

  • The grid. One square for each area of life, with one or two pictures in each. Clear and easy to update.
  • The centre and circle. Your most important dream or your word of the year in the middle, and everything else around it.
  • The timeline. Four columns for the four quarters of 2027, with each dream placed where you plan to act on it.

Make your 2027 vision board with someone

A shared board is a good way to start the year with a partner, friends or family. Each person dreams separately first, then you put what overlaps in the middle.

Paper or app?

Choose the one you will look at most. Paper is slower to make and hard to ignore on a wall. A board on your phone goes everywhere, is easy to change as the year changes, and can be shared.

Keep it alive all year

Most vision boards are forgotten by February. A few minutes a month keeps yours working.

  • January: take the first step on every dream. However small. Momentum matters more than size.
  • Each month: check in. Tick off what you did and choose the next step for each dream.
  • April, July and October: edit. Remove what no longer matters and add what does.
  • December: look back. See what came true, and start your 2028 board with what you learned.

Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

When should I make my 2027 vision board?

Any time from November to January works well. Late December and early January are the most popular, because you are already reflecting on the year. If you miss January, make it anyway: a board made in February still covers most of 2027.

What should I put on a 2027 vision board?

Pictures and words for one or two specific dreams in each area of life you care about: travel, home, career and money, health, relationships, growth and fun. Add a word for the year and a first step beside each dream.

How many goals should a 2027 vision board have?

Between six and twelve is a good range. That is one or two dreams in four to six areas. More than that and the board becomes a wish list that is hard to act on.
