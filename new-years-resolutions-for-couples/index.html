How to set New Year’s resolutions as a couple

Most resolutions fade by February. Doing them together helps, as long as you set them up well.

  1. Look back first. What were your best moments of 2026? What do you want more of, and less of?
  2. Choose three to five, not twenty. A short list you keep beats a long list you forget.
  3. Make them small and specific. “Be healthier” becomes “walk together after dinner.”
  4. Give each one a first step and a day. Decide what you’ll do this week.
  5. Check in every month. Celebrate what’s working and drop what isn’t, without blame.

Resolutions to feel closer

Small rituals do more for a relationship than grand gestures.

  • One proper date night every week
  • Phones away at dinner
  • A ten-minute check-in every Sunday
  • Say thank you for one specific thing each day
  • Go to bed at the same time twice a week
  • Take a photo together every month
  • Learn each other’s stress signals, and what helps

Money resolutions for couples

Money is easier when you’re looking at the same picture.

  • A monthly money date, with snacks
  • One shared savings goal with a date
  • Pay off a debt together and celebrate it
  • Agree a “talk first” amount for big purchases
  • Build an emergency fund you both feel calm about
  • Plan next year’s big trip and start the pot now

Health resolutions to do together

It’s easier to keep going when someone else is lacing up their shoes too.

  • A daily walk together
  • Cook at home four nights a week
  • Train for a race or a long hike
  • Try one new class a month
  • A shared bedtime routine without screens
  • Meal-plan on Sundays

Adventure and fun resolutions

Give the year something to look forward to.

  • One new place every month, even if it’s local
  • Finish five things from your bucket list
  • Book the trip you’ve talked about for years
  • Say yes to one spontaneous weekend
  • Start a hobby neither of you has tried
  • Recreate your first date

Home and life-admin resolutions

The unglamorous ones that make everything else lighter.

  • Split the chores in a way that feels fair to both
  • A weekly ten-minute planning chat
  • Finish one home project, start to finish
  • Declutter one room a month
  • Keep one shared list for errands and bills

Resolutions that support each other

Not every goal has to be shared. Some of the best ones are about backing each other.

  • Each pick one personal goal the other cheers on
  • Read the same book and talk about it
  • Learn a language before your next trip
  • Volunteer together for a cause you both care about
  • Make a couples vision board for the year

Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

What are good New Year’s resolutions for couples?

The ones that are small, specific and shared: a weekly date night, a monthly money check-in, a daily walk, one new place each month, and one personal goal each that the other supports.

How many resolutions should a couple make?

Three to five is plenty. Choose one for connection, one practical one (money or home) and one for fun, and add more only when those have become habits.

How do we stick to our resolutions together?

Write them down where you both see them, attach a first step and a day to each, and check in once a month. Shared reminders and a little encouragement go a long way.

Should couples make resolutions together or separately?

Both. Shared resolutions build the relationship, and supporting each other’s personal goals builds trust. A good list has some of each.
